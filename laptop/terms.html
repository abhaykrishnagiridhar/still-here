What this is

Still here is a student prototype made for Innovation Mission 9, a 48-hour robotics hackathon. It is a demonstration, not a product or a service, and it comes from a small team, not a company.

Not a safety device

The rover is not a certified rescue or safety device. It must not be relied on in any situation where a person could be hurt. It was built to be tested with foam and wooden blocks and a small light.

Moving parts

The rover has wheels and an arm that move. Keep fingers, hair and loose clothing away while it is powered, and supervise it at all times. Stop it by cutting its power if anything looks wrong.

The control page

The manual control page is meant for the team's own rover on the team's own network. It does not give access to any other device, and you should not use it with hardware you do not own or have permission to operate.
