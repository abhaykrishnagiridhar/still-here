Project blueprint

The blueprint.

How the pieces fit, what a full run looks like, the order everything gets built in, and who owns what.

System

The body and the brain

The rover is the body. It sees, moves, and keeps a copy of the approved plan. The laptop is the brain. It finds the blocks, plans the order, and shows the operator what it is about to do.

Power

Two supplies, one ground

The power bank runs only the ESP32-S3. A protected 2S 18650 pack runs the servos and the drive motors. Motor starts and servo stalls pull sharp current spikes, and on a shared supply those make the voltage dip and reset the ESP32. Now the spikes stay on their own side, and the two grounds join at one point.

Violet is the power bank side, teal is the pack side, and amber parts take signals from one and power from the other. Parts, capacitor values and the test plan are in power.md in the repository.

One run

From the light to a clear path

The dashed loops are the adapt behaviours. If the operator link drops, the rover finishes the stored plan on its own and says so on screen.

Build order

Nothing moves on until the gate passes

The two-block test comes first: lift the top block off a two-block stack without disturbing the one underneath. A run passes when the bottom block moves less than 5 mm and turns less than 10 degrees. Eight passes out of ten opens the gate.

People

Four people, one owner per area

Each person edits their own files. The messages between laptop and rover are written down in one place, and changing them takes all four.
