Privacy.

Last updated 14 October 2026

Still here is a student robotics project built for Innovation Mission 9. This page explains what this website does with your information. The short version: it collects none.

What we collect

Nothing. The site has no accounts, no forms, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising. We do not know who visits it.

Fonts from Google

The pages load the Hanken Grotesk typeface from Google Fonts. When your browser fetches it, Google receives your IP address and browser details, as it does for any site that uses Google Fonts. We do not receive that information. Google's own privacy policy applies to it.

Where the site is hosted

Whoever hosts these pages, for example GitHub Pages, may keep ordinary server logs such as IP addresses and request times. We do not run our own logging and cannot see those logs.

The control page

The manual control page is not connected to anything yet. Once the rover link exists, it is designed to talk to the rover over the team's own local network. Video from the rover's camera goes to the operator's own computer and is not sent to us or to any third party.

Contact

Questions about this page can be raised as an issue in the project's GitHub repository.

Changes

If the site starts collecting anything, this page will be updated first and the date above will change.
